Trip Overview

Escape the heat and hustle of the city with our refreshing 3-day getaway to the "Queen of Hill Stations." Nestled in the Nilgiri Hills, Ooty offers a perfect blend of colonial charm, lush tea gardens, and crisp mountain air. This carefully curated itinerary ensures you experience the best of Ooty and neighboring Coonoor, from serene boat rides to panoramic viewpoints, all while traveling in comfort and style. Perfect for couples, families, and nature lovers seeking a quick, rejuvenating retreat.

Day-by-Day Journey

01

Chennai to Ooty

Early morning departure. Enjoy a scenic drive through the winding Ghat roads. Check-in, relax, and enjoy an evening stroll around Ooty Lake with optional boating.

Drive Ooty Lake
02

Ooty Exploration

Full day sightseeing including the vibrant Botanical Gardens, the panoramic Doddabetta Peak, and a visit to a local Tea Factory to taste fresh brews.

Botanical Garden Doddabetta
03

Coonoor to Chennai

Morning drive to Coonoor. Visit Sim's Park and enjoy views from Dolphin's Nose. Optional heritage train ride back to Mettupalayam, followed by the return journey home.

Sim's Park Heritage Train
